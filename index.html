OUR LOVE STORY

Unang Kape: A Love Brewed Slowly

June just wanted her usual barako fix on a rainy Makati morning — but fate had other plans. Enter July: cute, kind, and just as coffee-obsessed. One rainy day. One shared laugh. Boom — daily coffee turned into daily kilig.
From pan de sal to late-night tawagan, their love story brewed slowly, like the perfect cup. Fast forward three years, July popped the question in the same café where it all started — with a sketchbook full of memories and a heartfelt "Gusto kitang makasama habang buhay."
They will tie the knot in a cozy Tagaytay garden, surrounded by love, laughter, and lola’s happy tears. No grand ballroom, just two hearts, one vow, and yes — great coffee. 💍❤️☕

    🗺️ Know the Schedule & Locations:
    Double-check the ceremony and reception addresses—they may be in different places.
    Leave early to allow time for traffic, parking, or getting lost (especially if unfamiliar with the area).
    Use a GPS app and download the maps in advance in case of poor signal.

    🚘 Transportation Tips:
    Carpool or rideshare if possible—parking may be limited at the church or garden.
    Check parking instructions ahead of time (some churches have designated lots; some gardens might not).
    Designate a driver if you plan to drink at the reception.
    If provided, take advantage of shuttles or group transport arranged by the couple.

    🧳 For Out-of-Town Guests:
    Stay at the suggested hotel(s) for convenience—group rates may apply.
    Pack wedding-appropriate attire and extras like bug spray, sunscreen, and a rain plan (small umbrella or poncho).
    Bring a printed invite or digital copy with times and addresses.

    📱 Tech Prep:
    Charge your phone fully and bring a portable charger if you'll be taking photos or using GPS.
    Save key contacts (wedding planner, group chat, other guests) in case you need help.

    💡 Bonus Tips:
    Eat a light snack before the ceremony to hold you over until the reception.
    Respect any quiet zones or unplugged ceremony requests.
    Be prepared for weather changes—check the forecast the morning of.
